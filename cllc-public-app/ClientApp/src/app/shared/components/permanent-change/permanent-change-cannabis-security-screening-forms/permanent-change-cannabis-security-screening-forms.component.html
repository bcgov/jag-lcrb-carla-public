Cannabis Screening Forms

Newly added associates for a cannabis licence or authorization are required to submit forms . Cannabis retail store licence or authorization applications cannot be processed until all required materials have been received.

The screening for associates considers the associate within the context of a larger organizational structure.

Please provide the documents as outlined on this page .

Police Information Check (may be referred to as Criminal Record and Judicial Matters Check outside of British Columbia)

Each associate must provide a complete Police Information Check.

A maximum of 20 files can be uploaded. If you need to upload more than 20 files, please contact LCRBCannabis@gov.bc.ca.

Cannabis Screening Forms
